Continuum Flexible Robotic Arm for Fruit Harvesting

Co-engineer

Result

Delivered a functional 2-DOF robotic arm prototype and integrated wireless control with an Arduino plus ESP-NOW communication setup for remote operation.

Contents

This project covered TPU-based structural prototyping, CAD modeling in Fusion 360, control-system implementation, and mobile-platform integration for maneuverability in harvesting scenarios.
